# Vision

> **Document type:** Vision · **Status:** Accepted (v1, 2026-10-02) · Kept deliberately short for the MVP.

## Problem

On an owner-run construction project, close-out work runs on two loops: correcting problems in built work, and instructing before work happens so problems don't occur. Today these live in spreadsheets, PDFs, photos and chat messages. What was agreed, what was built, what was measured and who must act next are hard to reconstruct and hard to share.

## Product

**BuiltBasis** is a lightweight construction-control application: one place to record, classify, measure, decide, evidence and track:

- **Quality Issues** — problems in built work;
- **Detail Clarifications** — instructions agreed before building;
- **Tasks** — other work to track.

Distinctive from generic snag-list apps: structured **measurements** with automatic comparisons, explicit **decisions and instructions**, and a bilingual (English/Greek) interface built for site use on a phone.

## Audience

v1: the project owner controls records. Architects, contractors and subcontractors view individual records through read-only links, or log in with per-record permission to upload evidence and/or add Log entries. Later: owner-side project managers and consultancy clients.

## Scope of v1

See `docs/designs/2026-10-02-v1-records-design.md`. Out of scope: other users editing record fields or existing content, offline mode, AI, procurement and commercial workflows, inspections and checklists.

## Success for v1

- The owner runs the live Gennadi 822A close-out from BuiltBasis instead of the spreadsheet tracker.
- Any record can be shared as a read-only link or an A3 page with a QR code.
- The application is usable on a phone on site, in Greek and in English.
